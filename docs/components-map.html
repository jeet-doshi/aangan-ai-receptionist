CASE 03 — AANGAN STUDIO AI RECEPTIONISTEvery phone enquiry answered in minutes, day or night · Jeet Doshi · Mesa School of Business
TRIGGERINPUTCONTEXTPROCESSINGOUTPUT
CALLER
(New enquiry or
existing client)
 Calls the studio, any hour. About 1 in 3 calls arrive outside 10am–7pm, when no one answers today. Says name, project type, size, site location, timeline; budget only if they volunteer it; may ask the price; or complains about an ongoing project. Knows what they want, but not whether it fits the studio's scope, area or timelines. Answers one direct follow-up question if project, location or timeline is unclear. Hears the right reply: next step to a consultation, an honest graceful decline, or an urgent acknowledgement. Never a price.
NIKHIL &
DESIGN TEAM
(Founder + 14 designers)
 — — Nikhil's rules, written down: qualified.md (5 criteria), services.md (scope, Pune/PCMC, timelines), pricing.md (no numbers). Humans decide only what the system flags: senior person takes escalations; designer confirms timeline-conflict leads. Designer calls back with full context, no re-asking. Nikhil sees calls, funnel and cost per lead on the dashboard instead of counting by hand.
AANGAN
RECEPTIONIST APP
(Next.js on Vercel)
 Vaani post-call webhook arrives LIVE, or a simulated call from the demo form (always labelled). Verifies the secret; keeps only the post-call event; normalises fields ("3 BHK" → full home, "Banner" → Baner, ms → s). The studio rules coded in: service-area list, excluded project types, 3,000 sq ft limit, 6-week lead time, the approved pricing line. ① Escalation check first → ② 5 criteria pass / fail / unclear → ③ one question, decline, or qualify → ④ pricing guard on every reply. Missing fields inferred from the summary and flagged "verify". Rules cost ₹0. Outcome: Qualified · Needs follow-up · Human review · Unqualified, with a handoff note and the caller's reply. Dashboard: KPIs, funnel, decline reasons, cost per call.
VAANI VOICE
(LLM voice agent)
 Picks up instantly, 24×7 LIVE Caller's speech → text transcript. Agent prompt: what the studio does, questions to ask, approved pricing line. Holds the conversation; after the call, writes a summary and extracts entities (name, location, project…). Sends call_postprocessing webhook: summary, transcript, entities, dispositions, duration.
SUPABASE
(Database)
 Every processed call. Full call record: caller, project, criteria results, outcome, escalation, reply, costs, handoff status. All past calls: 19 case-transcript replays (demo), simulated calls, live Vaani calls, each labelled. Stores the record; updates handoff status once Telegram confirms. Feeds the dashboard and its filters (date, source, outcome, escalations).
TELEGRAM
(Bot API)
 Fires on a qualified lead or an urgent escalation. Handoff note: caller, project, location, timeline, budget, decision-maker, criteria, reason. Design team chat (chosen over email: instant, shared, free). Delivers the message; Telegram API confirms delivery. 🟢 Qualified lead / 🔴 Urgent escalation on designers' phones. Marked "sent" only when confirmed.

✂ The Cut

Nikhil asked the system to "quote our standard per-square-foot pricing". pricing.md says no number may ever be quoted, so it was not built. Pricing questions get only the approved line and don't disqualify.

⚑ Flagged for Nikhil

Timeline rules conflict: services.md ≥ 6 weeks; qualified.md / T07 8–10 weeks; design + execution ≥ 11 weeks. < 6 wk declined, 6–10 wk sent to human review, not silently decided.

Honesty guardrails

Demo, simulated and live calls are always badged. "Sent" only on confirmed delivery. Costs shown as estimates until real Vaani rates are set. Inferred fields marked for the designer to verify.
